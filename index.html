What does unemployment rate mean?

The unemployment rate is the percentage of the labor force that is without work, available for work, and seeking employment.

Data source: World Bank indicator “Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate)”. Values are a simple public-data snapshot and may change as datasets are revised.
