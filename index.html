About this project

Understanding global demographic shifts

Fertility rates are one of the most consequential metrics in demography. This platform combines historical data from Our World in Data with an AI assistant to help researchers, students, and curious minds explore trends across Brazil, India, China, and beyond.

📊

Historical Data

Spanning over 70 years of fertility rate records sourced from Our World in Data, covering 190+ countries worldwide.

🤖

AI-Powered Insights

Ask natural-language questions about trends, causes, comparisons, and policy impacts — and get clear, contextual answers instantly.

🌍

Global Coverage

Deep focus on Brazil, India, and China — the world's three most populous nations — with broad global context for comparison.

The data

Where does the data come from?

All fertility data is sourced from Our World in Data, a project of the Global Change Data Lab — a registered UK non-profit (Charity No. 1186433). Their mission is to advance education on how global living conditions are changing through open, publicly available data.

🇧🇷

Brazil

From a fertility rate of over 6 in the 1960s to under 1.7 today — one of the fastest demographic transitions in Latin America.

🇮🇳

India

India has crossed the replacement threshold, with its rate declining from 5.9 in 1960 to approximately 2.0 in 2023.

🇨🇳

China

Dramatically shaped by the One Child Policy, China now faces one of the lowest fertility rates globally at around 1.0.
